My Favourite Places

Travelling is one of the best ways to explore the world, experience different cultures and create unforgettable memories. I love discovering beautiful destinations, historical places and peaceful locations. Here are some of my favourite places that I would love to visit.

1. Manali, Himachal Pradesh

Location: Himachal Pradesh, India

Manali is a beautiful hill station surrounded by snow-covered mountains, green valleys and rivers. It is famous for its natural beauty, adventure activities and peaceful atmosphere.

Why I want to visit:

  • Beautiful mountain views
  • Snowfall and pleasant weather
  • Adventure activities and sightseeing

2. Taj Mahal, Agra

Location: Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India

The Taj Mahal is one of the most beautiful monuments in the world. It is made of white marble and is a symbol of love. It is also recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Why I want to visit:

  • Magnificent architecture
  • Historical importance
  • Beautiful gardens and surroundings

3. Goa

Location: Goa, India

Goa is famous for its beautiful beaches, coastal views, Portuguese architecture and delicious food. It is a perfect destination for relaxation and enjoying holidays.

Why I want to visit:

  • Beautiful beaches and sunsets
  • Water sports and activities
  • Relaxing atmosphere

4. Kashmir

Location: Jammu and Kashmir, India

Kashmir is known for its breathtaking landscapes, beautiful lakes, gardens and snow-covered mountains. It is often called Paradise on Earth because of its natural beauty.

Why I want to visit:

  • Beautiful Dal Lake
  • Snow-covered mountains
  • Houseboat experiences and gardens

My Dream Travel Experience

I believe travelling gives us memories and experiences that stay with us forever. I would love to explore these destinations, experience their beauty and learn about different cultures.

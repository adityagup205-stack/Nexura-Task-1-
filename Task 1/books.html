2. The Alchemist

3. Rich Dad Poor Dad

Author: Robert Kiyosaki

Genre: Personal Finance

Favourite Recipes

My Favourite Recipes

Food is one of the best ways to bring happiness and creativity into our lives. I enjoy trying different dishes and exploring new flavours. Some recipes are special because of their taste, simplicity and the memories associated with them. Here are some of my favourite recipes.

1. Vegetable Pizza

Preparation Time: 15 minutes

Cooking Time: 15 minutes

Category: Italian Food

Ingredients

  • Pizza base
  • Pizza sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Chopped capsicum
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Oregano and chilli flakes

Cooking Instructions

  1. Take a pizza base and spread pizza sauce on it.
  2. Add chopped vegetables and grated cheese.
  3. Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes.
  4. Bake it until the cheese melts.
  5. Cut into slices and serve hot.

2. Paneer Butter Masala

Preparation Time: 15 minutes

Cooking Time: 25 minutes

Category: Indian Food

Ingredients

  • 200 grams paneer
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • Butter
  • Fresh cream
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt and spices

Cooking Instructions

  1. Heat butter in a pan.
  2. Add onions, tomatoes and ginger-garlic paste.
  3. Cook the mixture and prepare a smooth gravy.
  4. Add spices, paneer cubes and fresh cream.
  5. Cook for a few minutes and serve hot.

3. Chocolate Milkshake

Preparation Time: 5 minutes

Cooking Time: No cooking required

Category: Beverage

Ingredients

  • 1 glass chilled milk
  • 2 tablespoons chocolate syrup
  • 2 scoops chocolate ice cream
  • Sugar as required
  • Chocolate chips

Preparation Instructions

  1. Pour chilled milk into a blender.
  2. Add chocolate syrup and ice cream.
  3. Blend everything until smooth.
  4. Pour into a glass.
  5. Garnish with chocolate chips and serve chilled.
